Official Rental Terms

SACH CARS Policy & Guidelines

Transparent, fair, and hassle-free rental terms for your trip across Kerala.

📄
Required Documents
  • Indian Nationals: Original Driving License (1+ yr old) & Aadhaar Card / Passport.
  • Foreign Nationals: Valid International Driving Permit (IDP) + Passport copy.
  • Digital A4 sheets or expired licenses are strictly not accepted.
⏱️
Rental & Hours
  • Minimum Rental: 12 Hours for local / round trips. Special monthly rates (>30 days).
  • Operating Hours: Pickups & drops closed between 12:00 Midnight and 5:00 AM.
  • Grace Period: 1 Hour grace period provided; late return fine ₹300 + hourly charges apply.
💳
Security Deposit Refund
  • Refundable security deposit collected at delivery.
  • Processed & refunded within 7 working days post return.
  • FASTag toll charges, speed advisories, and vehicle inspection deducted if applicable.
🚨
Speed Advisory (80 km/h)
  • All vehicles monitored via GPS with an 80 km/h speed limit advisory as per Kerala MVD.
  • Fine of ₹1,500 charged per overspeed violation event.
  • Ensures maximum road safety on Kerala highways and hilly terrain.
🚚
Doorstep & Airport Delivery
  • Free Delivery: Within a 5 km radius of SACH Cars depots.
  • Cochin Airport (COK): Flat ₹300 one-way terminal pickup/drop fee.
  • One-Way Drop: Multi-location drops across Kerala districts & states supported.
❌
Cancellation Policy
  • > 24 Hours prior to trip: 100% full booking refund.
  • < 24 Hours prior to trip: 50% booking amount refund.
  • Non-production of original documents treated as late cancellation (no refund).

Frequently Asked Questions

Rental Knowledge Base

What documents are required to rent a self-drive car? ➕
Indian Nationals require an Original Driving License (held for at least 1 year) and an Aadhaar Card or Passport. Foreign Nationals require an International Driving Permit (IDP), country driving license, and passport copy.
When and how is the security deposit refunded? ➕
Security deposits are processed and refunded within 7 working days post vehicle return after verifying FASTag toll usage, speed limit advisories, and vehicle physical inspection.
What is the speed limit policy in Kerala? ➕
All SACH CARS vehicles are equipped with GPS tracking set to an 80 km/h speed limit advisory. Violating the speed limit attracts a fine of ₹1,500 per instance.
What is the cancellation policy? ➕
Cancellations made more than 24 hours before trip start get a 100% full refund. Cancellations made within 24 hours get a 50% refund.
